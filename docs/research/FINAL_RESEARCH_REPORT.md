# CFP-FishMOT research evidence report

**IN_PROGRESS — not a final scientific conclusion.** Updated 2026-10-12. No independent method gain or SOTA claim exists yet.

Current evidence: migration/preflight checks pass; historical metric replay recovers HOTA 31.0911 / AssA 19.6192 / DetA 49.5748. Fifteen requested reference repositories were inspected at pinned commits, with partial-training and license restrictions documented. A six-video/train two-video/dev association protocol is frozen, with historical detector exposure disclosed. Twenty-four infrastructure/causality/diagnostic tests pass. Full cu117 detector inference reproduces all 15,985 frames' cache arrays exactly and all seven historical track files byte-for-byte, including the historical TrackEval results.

New full detector inference on the inherited cu118 runtime gives HOTA 30.907 / AssA 19.371 / DetA 49.580 / IDF1 34.684 / IDSW 568. Restoring cu117/cuDNN 8.5 resolves this drift on the full dataset. cu118/cu117 full elapsed times were 17m50.55s/18m12.98s, including decoding/tracking/evaluation; these are not strict-online latencies. Frozen shared ImageNet ResNet50 features have now been extracted for 14,528 paired GT crops in 40.02s. Exploratory legacy-input identity retrieval shows equal fusion improves BT-005 but harms SN-013; see PHASE_I_MODALITY_IDENTITY_DIAGNOSIS.md for exact numbers and limits. Fish-finetuned ReID, trained CMUE/PLIM, fair tracker comparisons, three-seed ablations and final frozen evaluation remain unperformed. Thus no answer yet exists for CMUE vs quality gate or PLIM vs EMA.

Research problem: conditional identity usefulness of constructed IR under limited representation/model capacity, plus causal latent identity prediction and separate reliable memory writes. Four recovered original generators are locally audited and their SHA256 hashes match NAS. Whole-sequence median backgrounds and adaptive strategy selection use future RGB frames, so historical full pipelines are not fully causal. A separate current/past-only EMA version is implemented and tested, preserving the fixed response parameters. Cached geometry and component lookup optimizations preserve exact pixels on twelve sampled full-resolution frames; standalone steady-state generation is still about 0.30–0.34s/frame, which must be counted in end-to-end costs. Complete causal data/caches and matched RGB-history controls remain required before online conclusions. Two duplicate frame/identity keys in SN-013 have four ambiguous boxes excluded from identity samples and masked as official distractors in versioned derived GT; original data is preserved. Proposed contributions must survive capacity/evidence/compute-matched baselines and interventions; code alone is insufficient.

Actual artifacts are linked through the experiment registry and FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md. Final goal remains active. This document will be rewritten around completed evidence after the declared research phases, including failures and any unsupported hypotheses.
