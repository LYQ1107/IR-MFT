# IR-MFT Phase VI research report

**Status: FINAL_SCOPED_CONCLUSION.** The measured evidence supports retaining the strong ordinary GRU/current-measurement baseline and not deploying the candidate modules. The conclusion is scoped to the frozen detector/ReID exposure and six-video association protocol; it is not a claim that all recoverability mechanisms are impossible.

## 1. Research question and exploration route

Can strictly past accepted observations and the current candidate set identify recoverable ambiguity, separate plausible identities and protect stable memory? The route was baseline reproduction → actual-error taxonomy → risk/ordinary controls → independent K2 → candidate attention/EMA controls → video-excluded state regeneration → write-budget controls → raw relational prediction → bounded corrections → repeated seeds and full ABC interactions. Failed models were preserved and used to design discriminating experiments; no fixed trial count or HOTA threshold ended research.

## 3. A: risk decisions and memory protection

A uses21 observable edge features: prototype/history similarities, margins/entropy, IoU/motion, confidence, age/gap, stage, crowding, ordinary cost and cross-track margin. Same-input MLP, candidate pooling, learned cost threshold, history removal and calibrated untrained controls separate learning from simple gating. Unknown physical labels remain excluded from known-wrong supervision. Direct association replacement/rejection harms FN/MOTA; preserving current assignment/output while deferring suspect writes is safer. Absolute calibrated gates fail to transfer to regenerated producer states. Fixed per-stage floor(10% of actually matched scored edges) makes the action budget explicit; compare confidence, margin, entropy and seeded random ordering. Small pools cannot reject an edge and use ordinary writes. This is not a calibrated posterior of recoverability.

## 4. B: candidate-conditioned prediction and teacher/student mechanism

The initial109,186-parameter candidate attention/EMA family uses real accepted histories, current candidates, explicit NULL, masks and known-only contamination supervision. Ordinary candidate MLP/GRU/attention, two-layer Transformer105,153 and an ID-dictionary decoder108,416 are local controls. The ID decoder is MOTIP-inspired rather than a faithful official MOTIP implementation; whole-frame grouping/loss information differ, so no claim of defeating MOTIP is supported.

The subsequent relation family operates on raw RGB cosine/candidate margins, actual first3+recent5 tokens, time and current geometry. Training-only EMA encodes clean current/future identity-to-candidate relations; future targets have no current fallback. Student forward/export has no GT, future crop or teacher. Cached full-history scalar channels are excluded from masked relation inputs. A real512-update EMA and exact model/optimizer/RNG epoch3-resume were verified. Known-negative missing-candidate augmentation increases NULL rejection but also wrongly rejects positives.

Unrestricted learned cost replacement creates large IDSW. The residual variant preserves the unmasked ordinary prior and limits supplemental changes to±0.05 inside native appearance eligibility. Masking applies only to the supplemental branch. It has4,389 parameters, zero-initialized correction, candidate-centered latent prediction and optional known-cue utility. Its own epoch3 stop/resume matches continuous model/EMA/optimizer/all RNG/order/loss/guard exactly, excluding only Git provenance.

## 5. C: bounded causal alternative explanations

K2 branches on a legal near-tied pair swap with ambiguity0.05, discounted current matching cost0.9 and optional fixed0.02 hysteresis. Each explanation owns motion, accepted/early history, writer evidence, counters and RNG. Current frames use the current winner; future observations can change future decisions but cannot revise emitted history. Branch score is a heuristic, not a posterior. Duplicate-output, bounded-width, birth reuse, isolation and nested quota/alternate assignment guards are tested. K1/all-disabled complete outputs reproduce every six-video outer baseline byte-for-byte.

## 6. Difference from RIS, PLIM and Fish-JEPA

RIS weights historical reliability into a prototype; PLIM/Fish-JEPA predict a single identity representation before comparing candidates. Phase VI conditions on current candidate relations, separates memory action from output acceptance, and tests explicit alternative trajectory states. Added complexity alone is not innovation: teacher, learned risk and branching need independent evidence.

## 7. Recoverability diagnosis and scoped oracle

Known wrong rows often retain a current-stage positive but require score/acceptance repair. Geometry eligibility is appearance blending, not a hard Hungarian mask. Detection absence, stage absence, unknown labels, weak past cues and one-to-one competition are separated. An unknown past token never proves irrecoverability. Joint GT interventions on fixed states reduce naive independent reranking opportunity; they are analysis-only and not a tracking ceiling or inference mechanism. PHASE_VI_RECOVERABILITY_ANALYSIS.md and PHASE_VI_JOINT_ORACLE_MEASURED.json retain full two-axis/category/episode evidence.

## 14. Teacher, risk and branching novelty audit

EMA necessity: unproven; same-input noEMA performs nearly identically. Candidate risk necessity: unproven; margin and ordinary thresholds explain much of the benefit. K2 necessity: unproven; C is marginal and lowers AB. AB complementarity was tested with repeated initialization, noEMA and fixed-margin controls; the identity-coordinate expansion does not establish a deployment-level candidate gain. Three-seed baseline variance does not establish scene generalization. No CVPR-ready novelty or guaranteed recovery claim follows from these results.
