Status: video split frozen before new association outcomes, 2026-10-12. Current machine contract: `configs/cfp/protocol_v2.json`; v1 is preserved. V2 fixes malformed GT handling before any dev method evaluation, without changing videos/targets/search budgets. Actual data root: `/data3/liuyeqiang/datasets/mft26-ir`. ## Data integrity and split

All 32,085 training and 15,985 historical test RGB/IR file pairs exist and are nonempty. 102 sampled pairs decoded with equal 1080×1920 dimensions. This checks naming/dimensions, not pixel-level registration or every JPEG decode. Frames and GT ranges are continuous. Annotation JSON hashes and per-video GT hashes are recorded in the machine contract. Training JSON contains 263,666 boxes; test JSON has no annotations, and evaluation uses sequence GT text.

The two development videos are the median-duration members within repeated BT/SN prefix groups. MSK/PF singleton groups remain in training. This choice precedes feature/association outcomes. Training-only audit uses all GT boxes for overlap statistics and 24 uniformly spaced paired frames per video for intensity statistics. The fractions of GT targets overlapping another GT box by IoU >0.1 are 0.465 for BT-005 and 0.462 for SN-013. These are box-overlap proxies, not annotated occlusion or species labels. Prefixes do not establish species, scenes, original recording separation, or physical fish separation; those metadata remain unverified. The 49-ID MSK scene is absent from primary development. Supplemental complete-video folds include MSK/PF; do not pool fold metrics as if they were independent test data.

Identity key is `(sequence, track_id)`. Identity labels are assigned only within each training partition. Same numeric ID in another video is a different label. No frame slicing across train/dev. Features/teacher fitting, hard-negative mining and calibration never use historical test labels. Cross-fitting groups are explicitly listed in the contract; an out-of-fold video's teacher must not be trained on its identity crops. Freeze teachers before generating counterfactual targets and record their hashes.

## Existing contamination and provenance

Historical V3 and visible-only weights were trained on all eight original training videos. Holding out two videos protects new identity/association fitting only; the detector has seen their images. Describe this as video-heldout association development under a fixed previously trained detector. Do not claim independent full-system development. A future detector retrained on six videos would be a separate protocol and require separate caches.

The seven historical test videos previously selected tracker hyperparameters and the V3 checkpoint. They are historical reproducibility/frozen final reporting material, not a blind test. No new architecture, threshold, checkpoint or failure-repair selection uses them. Obtaining new external videos is desirable but none is currently confirmed.

**IR provenance update from the user:** these images are rule-based pseudo-thermal response maps derived from visible RGB, not measured thermal radiation/temperature or a learned diffusion generator. Green/Lab local contrast, foreground/background suppression, gamma, wavelet denoising and INFERNO coloring produce the single-frame variants. SN train/test and PF-002 use a temporal variant whose background is the pixel median of up to 80 uniformly sampled frames from the whole sequence. This introduces future-frame information into early IR frames. MSK unified_v2 source remains to audit. The supplied sequence directory matches are based on first/middle/last frame hashes, not exhaustive provenance. Exact metadata and former source paths are in `configs/cfp/ir_provenance.json`; original scripts have not yet been inspected on this server.

**Strict online input protocol:** historical detection/replay results use legacy pseudo-IR, including noncausal preprocessing, and must be labeled accordingly. They cannot establish a fully causal complete system. Build a separate current/past-only IR dataset and caches before strict-online full-system claims; fix one causal transform/config for each comparison rather than choosing a favorable generator by dev outcome. All methods in a causal Protocol A use exactly the same causal-input detector cache, distinct from legacy-cache exploratory Protocol A. Identity encoders and memory must also receive causal IR. Compare same-RGB-history/background/contrast controls so past background processing is not mistaken for an independent sensor. Conditional information claims concern the available RGB history; legacy IR derived from future RGB is not necessarily limited to the information in a single current RGB image.

## Malformed identity annotation handling

Identity sample construction found duplicate keys in both JSON and GT: SN-013 `(967,4)` has two boxes and `(1001,6)` has two spatially distinct boxes. Raw TrackEval rightly rejects repeated IDs in one frame. Never silently pick one box or modify original annotations. V2 excludes all four ambiguous boxes from identity samples. A derived GT copy assigns those boxes unique dummy IDs and official MOT distractor class 8, so official preprocessing ignores ambiguous GT and predictions assigned to them. Other boxes retain original labels. All compared methods use this same derived GT and recorded source/derived hashes. The corresponding four boxes cannot be used to train/diagnose ReID; video and identity counts remain unchanged. Derived files live at `/data3/liuyeqiang/IR-MFT_assets/cfp/protocol_v2/gt`; pass that root explicitly to the unified evaluator. Historical raw test is unchanged and retains exact reproduction protocol. This is an input-integrity repair before association outcomes, not a metric-driven label change.

## Metrics and comparable experiments

Use official TrackEval commit `12c8791b303e0a0b50f753af204249e622d0281a`, unmodified metric code. The historical fork's `hota.py`, `clear.py` and `identity.py` are byte-identical to this commit. `tools/cfp_evaluate.py` prepares standard MOT directories, validates finite boxes/frame order/unique IDs, and saves raw official arrays plus percent summaries. The old loader's removed NumPy builtin aliases are restored only in the evaluator process. Fish are mapped to class 1 (TrackEval calls this pedestrian). Keep marked=1/class=1 GT; no distractor categories are present. HOTA averages official alpha thresholds 0.05:0.05:0.95. CLEAR/Identity use IoU 0.5. Aggregation is official `combine_sequences`, not an unweighted average of video percentages. Never infer AssA from HOTA.

Protocol A: exactly the same V3-E3 post-NMS detection boxes/scores/order for every association method. Encoders use those boxes online. Report detector proposals and tracker-emitted boxes separately: changed association can change track emissions and therefore DetA even with frozen proposals. Protocol B: methods' own full pipelines; clearly separate from A. Replay caches contain every frame including empty frames and must identify detector weight, input normalization/size/channel order, confidence/NMS/precision, source commit and annotation hashes. Historical caches without a sidecar are only historical artifacts; their source is not fully proven by a matching filename.

Report HOTA, AssA, DetA, LocA, AssRe/AssPr, DetRe/DetPr, MOTA, IDF1, IDP/IDR, IDSW, FP/FN, Frag, MT/ML, per-video values and combined results. Historical motmetrics outputs are not substituted for TrackEval counts. Record full precision arrays; rounded historical summaries have ±0.0005-point tolerance. Same detections and GT should produce bit-identical replay tracks for baseline fallback.

## Selection, budgets and causal constraints

Initial baselines plus at most three evidence-directed repair rounds per core hypothesis. Record unsuccessful trials. Tune only on development within a declared comparable budget. Select checkpoints by dev HOTA, then AssA, then IDF1. Three final seeds: 0, 1, 2. Aspirational differences against the strongest reproducible A baseline: HOTA +1pp, AssA +2pp, IDF1 +2pp; require independently useful CMUE and PLIM ablations, no increased IDSW, MOTA drop ≤0.5pp, and full-system latency ≤2× baseline. These are prospective targets, not observed gains; report tradeoffs and negative results honestly. With only two dev videos, video-bootstrap uncertainty has limited resolution; do not claim significance from many correlated frames.

At frame t: predict from accepted history <t, encode detections at t, score/assign, update geometry, then optionally write identity memory at t. No future observations, GT online inputs, offline interpolation or end-of-video optimization. Train predictive targets may use future GT crops with stop-gradient; inference may not. Teacher-forced identity tests and actual tracker tests are separate.

Measure cached-association latency separately from end-to-end decoding/detector/encoders/memory/assignment. Synchronize GPU for measured boundaries; report warm-up, batching, p50/p95, parameters, FLOPs (unsupported operators disclosed), peak memory, hardware and versions. This project may use at most four simultaneous GPUs; currently physical GPU 2 only, avoid historical reserved GPU 0/1 and other occupied jobs.
