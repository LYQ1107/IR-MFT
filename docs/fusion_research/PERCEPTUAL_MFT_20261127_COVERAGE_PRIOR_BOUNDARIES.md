Measurement-driven adaptive birth modeling predates this project. Ristic, Clark, Vo and Vo distinguish persistent and newborn targets in PHD/CPHD filtering, using received measurements to adapt births. Consequently, the general idea of adapting births to unassigned measurements is prior work. Our confidence-mass ratio is a heuristic, not a PHD/CPHD intensity or a calibrated expected object count. Berry, Bucci and Schmidt also describe online measurement-driven adaptive births with multiple sensors, applied to passive TDOA/FDOA tracking. Thus, adding multiple information sources to birth modeling is also an established direction. Their physically distinct measurement setting does not establish a benefit for an RGB-derived pseudo-IR view; that remains an experimental question here. The author abstract was checked. [Passive multi-target adaptive-birth paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.02396).
